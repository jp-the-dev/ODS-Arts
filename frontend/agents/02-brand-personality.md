## Brand Voice Attributes

### 1. Quiet Confidence
We do not shout. We do not use urgency tactics or pushy CTAs. We present with calm authority. The work speaks. We contextualize it.

> ❌ "BUY NOW! Limited stock! Don't miss out!"
> ✅ "Each piece is made to order. Take your time."

### 2. Poetic Restraint
Copy is sparse but weighted. Every word earns its place. We favor evocative language that conjures feeling, not just features.

> ❌ "High-quality wooden frame with UV-resistant glass."
> ✅ "Solid walnut. UV-shielded. Built to outlast the moment it holds."

### 3. Intimate Intelligence
We speak to people who appreciate quality — they don't need it explained, just validated. Tone is that of a trusted advisor, not a salesperson.

> ❌ "Our frames are made of the best materials."
> ✅ "We source our walnut from sustainable mills in Northern Europe. You'll feel the difference."

### 4. Emotionally Anchored
We acknowledge that what we frame matters deeply. Our language honors that weight without being sentimental or overwrought.

> ❌ "Capture your precious memories forever!"
> ✅ "Some moments deserve more than a screen. Give them a wall."

## Copywriting Principles

1. **Lead with feeling, follow with fact** — Emotion first, specification second.
2. **White space is not empty** — Short copy signals confidence.
3. **No exclamation marks** — Enthusiasm is conveyed through specificity, not punctuation.
4. **One idea per sentence** — Clarity is a form of luxury.
5. **Avoid passive voice** — We craft. We source. We deliver.
6. **Use "you" and "your"** — Keep the customer central.
