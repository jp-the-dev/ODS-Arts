# ODSArts — UI Moodboard Description

> A textual moodboard — each element described as if you're looking at a physical pinboard covered in references, swatches, and inspiration.

---

## The Moodboard in Words

Imagine a large, dark pinboard — the surface is dark walnut-stained cork. On it, placed with intention and care, are the following:

---

### 🖼️ Reference Image 1 — Hasselblad Website (Your Reference)
A full-screen obsidian background with the camera resting in dramatic warm spotlight. The text "HASSELBLAD MASTERS 2026" in serif, wide-tracking, gold-toned. The entire composition feels like looking at a luxury watch advertisement. This is the **tonal anchor** for ODSArts — this exact darkness, this weight, this silence.

**What we borrow:** Background depth, warm spotlight feel, serif headline confidence, minimal navigation.

---

### 🖼️ Reference Image 2 — Aesop Store Interior
Terracotta walls, warm Edison bulb lighting, apothecary bottles arranged with deliberate spacing. Everything is brown, amber, and cream. Nothing is plastic. The air feels thick with meaning.

**What we borrow:** Warmth palette, materiality, the feeling of stepping into a curated world, editorial stillness.

---

### 🖼️ Reference Image 3 — Apple Product Page Scroll
One message per scroll. The product appears against pure space. The copy is 8 words maximum per section. The animation reveals the object as if light is slowly coming up in a theatre.

**What we borrow:** Restraint in copy, pacing of scroll, theatrical product reveal, single-focus sections.

---

### 🖼️ Reference Image 4 — Aura Frames Lifestyle Photography
Photographs of real homes — warm wood floors, morning light, a frame on a gallery wall with a candid family moment inside it. The feeling is not "look at this product" but "imagine your life with this."

**What we borrow:** Emotional storytelling, real-home context, lifestyle over studio.

---

### 📐 Layout Reference — Gallery Wall

A photograph of a high-end gallery — MOMA or White Cube.
- White (warm white, not clinical white) walls with massive breathing room around each artwork
- Single work of art centered on each wall — dramatic negative space
- Lighting from above, angled — sculptural shadow from the frame itself

**What we borrow:** The confidence to use space. The courage to not fill every pixel.

---

### 💡 Lighting Reference

A pinned photograph of a single picture frame in a dark room:
- The frame is illuminated by a single warm gallery spotlight
- The light catches the wood grain, the edge of the glass
- Outside the light: pure darkness
- Inside the light: warm amber, cream, gold

This image represents the **photographic and UI lighting philosophy** — spot-lit product, dark surrounding space.

## The Single Mood Sentence

> If ODSArts were a room, it would be a **private gallery in a converted stone building** — warm Edison light, dark walls, silence, and one photograph on each wall that makes you stop breathing for a moment.

---

## Anti-Moodboard (What We Are NOT)

These images are marked with an ❌ on the moodboard:

- ❌ A bright white e-commerce page with 4 product grids above the fold
- ❌ A Canva template with gradient purple backgrounds
- ❌ Confetti and emojis in the CTA section
- ❌ Arial/Helvetica body text
- ❌ Drop shadows on everything
- ❌ A carousel banner with 3 discount offers
- ❌ Blue "Add to Cart" buttons
- ❌ Product photography on pure white backgrounds only
- ❌ Stock photography of "happy families"
